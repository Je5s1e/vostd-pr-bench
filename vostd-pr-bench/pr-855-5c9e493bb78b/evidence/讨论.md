## Remove the paging-layout axiom

Remove `PagingConstsTrait::axiom_current_paging_consts_hardcoded` and all of its uses. The layout facts required by the current page-table owner model become explicit proof obligations of `PageTableConfig`, proved by the user and kernel configurations. Index-range proofs derive their bounds from the generic paging configuration. This replaces an unchecked assumption with obligations that Verus checks for each implementation.
