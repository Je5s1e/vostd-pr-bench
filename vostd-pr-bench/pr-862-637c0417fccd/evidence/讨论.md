## Summary

`is_partition` placed pairwise disjointness and full coverage inside the per-part `forall`. With an empty `parts` set, that made the implication vacuously true even when `s` was nonempty, despite the union of no parts being empty.

Move pairwise disjointness and the union equality outside the per-part quantifier. Add `lemma_is_partition_empty_parts` to prove that an empty family partitions `s` exactly when `s` is empty.
