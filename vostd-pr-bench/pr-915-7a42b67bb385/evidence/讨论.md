The assumption holds for the setup ELF produced by the normal build pipeline. Both callers obtain `va` from its segment addresses. The linker script places the header at `0xff000` and text at `0x100000`. ELF32 addresses fit in `u32`; for ELF64, the checked `R_X86_64_32` relocation for `__executable_size` bounds the successfully linked image size. On the supported 64-bit host, these addresses satisfy the assumed range.
